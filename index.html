RESEARCH ARTICLE

Enhanced Passive ISAR Drone Surveillance Using Micro-Doppler-Assisted Multi-Bernoulli TBD Filtering

NAME: [الاسم هنا]
ID: [الـ ID هنا]
² Department of Electronics and Communication, Arab Academy for Science Technology and Maritime Transport, Cairo, Egypt
ABSTRACT – Passive Inverse Synthetic Aperture Radar (ISAR) systems provide an effective solution for covert drone surveillance in cluttered environments. However, tracking drones inside Doppler-blind zones (DBZs) remains challenging because target Doppler signatures overlap with stationary clutter under low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) conditions. This paper proposes a Micro-Doppler-Assisted Track-Before-Detect Multi-Bernoulli Filter (mD-TBD-MBF) for passive ISAR drone tracking in DBZ environments. The proposed framework combines micro-Doppler harmonic extraction, TBD processing, and MB Random Finite Set filtering to improve weak target detection and tracking continuity. An adaptive harmonic-guided particle redistribution mechanism is introduced to enhance clutter robustness and preserve target observability during Doppler-blind-zone crossings. Simulation results demonstrate improved detection probability, reduced false alarms, and enhanced trajectory estimation accuracy compared with conventional tracking filters.
